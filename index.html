How to play

  1. Bid. Each player bids on how many points their team will take this round, or passes. The highest bidder wins the bid.
  2. Pick up the nest and name trump. The bid winner adds the nest to their hand, discards the same number of cards, and names the trump color.
  3. Take tricks. Follow the color that was led if you can. The highest card wins the trick, unless someone plays trump.
  4. Count the points. If the bidding team takes at least what it bid, it scores its points. If not, it's set, and the bid comes off its score. The first team to the winning score wins.

The point cards in the official rules:

  • 5s: 5 points
  • 10s: 10 points
  • 14s: 10 points
  • Blackbird: 20 points
