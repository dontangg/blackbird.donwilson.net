Support

Blackbird! is a card game for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Robert Wilson. Here's how to get help or tell me what you think.

Email

Write to blackbirdcardgame@gmail.com about anything: a problem with the game, your credits or theme packs, your account, or privacy. Tell me which device you play on and, for a problem, what happened just before it. I read every email, and I'll reply as soon as I can.

Bugs and ideas

You can also report a bug or suggest an idea on the public issue list, where you can see what others have reported. Everything there is public, so don't include any personal information. You'll need a free GitHub account.

Your account and privacy

To delete your Blackbird account, see Delete your account. The privacy policy explains what the app collects and why.
